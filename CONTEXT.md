# Portion nutrition journal

Portion records reviewed meals and their nutrition sources so people can understand daily calories and macronutrients.

## Language

**Meal**: A dated collection of food or drink entries saved after review.

**Portion**: The total edible quantity attributed to one entry, expressed in grams for food or milliliters for a drink. Household measures can propose a portion but do not prove a measured weight.
_Avoid_: Auto-weighed amount

**Weighing state**: Whether food was raw/dry, cooked, or as packaged when its amount was measured. Preparation alone does not establish weighing state.

**AI estimate**: Nutrition proposed from a model's knowledge and visible assumptions, without a retrieved database record or verified manufacturer label.
_Avoid_: Verified AI nutrition

**Database record**: A retrieved nutrition entry with its original source identity and reported values, selected for a matching food and preparation. Retrieval does not prove that it matches a person's meal.

**Package label**: Nutrition and serving size entered from the user's product packaging. It retains its identity as user-entered information.

**Usual meal**: A reviewed reusable meal template whose quantities and sources can be edited before another log entry.

**Sample walkthrough**: An isolated demonstration with illustrative authored values, never evidence of live AI parsing or nutritional accuracy.
_Avoid_: Live AI demo
